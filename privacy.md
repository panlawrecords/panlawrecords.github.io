# Pan Law Records — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

Pan Law Records is an independent media project based in Paducah, Kentucky. Our programming includes local music, independent films, community media, and other creative work.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you access the Pan Law Records channel on Roku.

## 2. Information We Collect

Pan Law Records does not require viewers to create an account or provide personal information to watch programming through our channel.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly from viewers through the channel unless a feature or service we introduce in the future requires it. If our practices change, we will update this policy as appropriate.

## 3. Roku and Third-Party Services

Our channel operates on the Roku platform and may use third-party services to host, deliver, or display video content.

Roku may collect and process information about your device, use of its platform, viewing activity, and interactions with applications, in accordance with its own policies and applicable law.

Third-party hosting and delivery providers may also process technical information, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and access logs, when delivering video and other content.

Pan Law Records does not control the privacy practices of Roku or independent third-party service providers. Please review their respective privacy policies for information about their practices.

Roku's Privacy Policy is available at:
https://docs.roku.com/published/userprivacypolicy/en/us

## 4. How Information May Be Used

Information processed by platform and hosting providers may be used to deliver content, maintain service reliability, diagnose technical problems, protect systems against abuse, and measure usage.

Pan Law Records does not sell viewers' personal information. We do not use the channel to build personal advertising profiles or to send unsolicited marketing messages.

## 5. Data Retention and Security

Pan Law Records does not maintain a viewer account database through the channel. Technical logs, if generated by our hosting or delivery providers, may be retained according to those providers' policies and operational requirements.

We take reasonable steps to protect the services and information under our control. However, no internet-based service or method of data transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Pan Law Records does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the channel. Parents and guardians should consider the suitability of individual programs for children, as programming may cover a range of subjects and audiences.

Platform-level information collection is governed by the applicable platform policies and law.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information held by service providers, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain information.

For information collected by Roku, please use Roku's privacy resources and available privacy controls. For information directly controlled by Pan Law Records, contact us using the details below.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our channel, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the current version took effect.
